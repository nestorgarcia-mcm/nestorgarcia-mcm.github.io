References you can check,
project by project

For each project I ask the project manager or client representative I reported to for two things: a signed reference letter and a structured assessment of my work. Both are reviewed and approved by them before I share anything.

01

How it works

The same four steps for every reference, so a recruiter or client always receives the same kind of evidence.

Reference letter

On company letterhead and signed: my role, dates, the scope I managed and the reason the assignment ended. In Spanish and English.

Skills assessment

An online questionnaire of about ten minutes: a 1 to 5 rating for each skill, with room for a concrete example from the project.

Review and approval

The referee sees the generated document, corrects anything and approves it. Nothing is sent without that approval.

Shared on request

Letters and assessments go to recruiters and clients on request, with the referee's agreement to be contacted.

02

What is assessed

Twelve skills in three groups. The list is adjusted to my role on each project and to the role of the company I worked for: contractor, client representative or vendor specialist.

A · MANAGEMENT

Management & leadership

  • Team leadership · supervisors, welders and subcontractors
  • Planning & productivity · progress control and milestones
  • Decision-making · speed and judgement under pressure
  • Problem solving · shortages, clashes, scope changes
B · TECHNICAL

Technical, quality & safety

  • Technical knowledge · piping, welding, NDT, equipment
  • Quality (QA/QC) · weld book, punch list, releases
  • Health & safety · site safety culture and compliance
  • Digital tools & data · Power BI, material and site data control
C · ATTITUDE

Attitude & relationships

  • Effort & commitment · involvement and availability
  • Professionalism & integrity · reliability and ethics
  • Client communication · owner, engineering and contractors
  • Adaptability · response to project changes
03

Available references

References are personal documents with third-party contact details, so they are not published here.

Letters and completed assessments are sent on request. Tell me the role or project you are assessing me for and I will send the references that match it, with the referee's contact details where they have agreed to be contacted. Contact me or message me on LinkedIn.
04

For referees

If I have asked you for a reference, you will have received a personal link to your questionnaire by email or WhatsApp. It works on a phone or a computer, needs no account, and saves your answers on your device while you fill it in.

You only choose ratingsfrom drop-down lists; comments are optional.
You see the document firstand approve it before anything is sent.
You send it yourselfby email or WhatsApp, with the text already written.
